Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide the Service:

  • Account data: First name, last name, email address, and hashed password.
  • Practice data: Topics practiced, scores, time spent, and session history.
  • Uploaded files: Study materials you upload (processed and discarded — not stored long-term).
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To generate personalized AI practice problems and track your progress.
  • To send email verification codes and (if opted in) study reminders.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • To improve the Service based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns.

3. AI Processing & Third Parties

Practice questions and answer feedback are generated using the Anthropic Claude API. When you submit a question or upload a file, that content is sent to Anthropic's servers for processing under their Privacy Policy. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored in a secured database hosted on Railway.
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plain-text passwords.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • Access tokens are short-lived (15 minutes) to minimize exposure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data via Account Settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by emailing us.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails via Account Settings → Preferences.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use localStorage to store your authentication token and sessionStorage for guest session state. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

7. Children's Privacy

GED Study Guide is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us immediately and we will delete the account.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related requests or questions, email privacy@gedmathmaster.com.
